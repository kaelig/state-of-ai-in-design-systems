This report does not rank systems, and “AI-ready” is not a field in the data. What exists is

The rating measures how much of the machine-facing surface a system has built, not how well it
works. Nobody in this study published head-to-head quality numbers that would support a ranking, and
the maturity call is one rubric applied by one person. Read the caveats at https://state-of-ai-in-design-systems.netlify.app/methodology.md
